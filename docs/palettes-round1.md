### Validated coffee/food primaries from ui-ux-pro-max database:
- **Bakery/Cafe:** #92400E brown — "Warm brown + cream white"
- **Recipe & Cooking:** #9A3412 terracotta — "Warm terracotta + fresh green"
- **Brewery/Winery:** #7C2D12 burgundy — "Deep burgundy + craft gold"
- **Food Delivery:** #EA580C orange — "Appetizing orange + trust blue"
- **Restaurant:** #DC2626 red — "Appetizing red + warm gold"
- **Grocery:** #059669 green — "Fresh green + food amber"
- **Agriculture:** #15803D green — "Earth green + harvest gold"

### NOT validated for food/coffee:
- Dark blues, slates, cobalt (cool colors clash with warm food vibe)
- Dark forest/pine/olive as food primaries (reserved for pharmacy/florist/plant-care in DB)
- Purple, pink (wrong semantic category)

### Shared across all palettes:
- Gold stars: #D4A017 or #C4862D
- Green "open" badge: #16A34A (for non-green primaries) or darker shade of primary green
- Destructive: #DC2626
